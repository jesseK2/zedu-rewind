What is Zedu?

Zedu is a communication and learning platform built for schools, bootcamps and training programmes. It combines structured channels, learning workflows and AI-powered support to help educators teach and learners stay engaged.

How does Zedu support students and learners?

Zedu supports learners with organized class spaces, timely reminders, searchable learning materials and quick help through AI assistants. Students can follow lessons, ask questions and track progress in one place.

Is Zedu difficult to set up for a school or bootcamp?

No. Zedu is designed for quick onboarding with simple setup steps, guided workspace creation and role-based controls. Most teams can start running cohorts or classes in a short time.

Can Zedu use our institution's information to answer questions?

Yes. You can connect your institutional content — course documents, policies and internal resources — so AI assistants can provide responses grounded in your own approved materials.

Is student support fully automated?

No. Zedu uses a hybrid model where AI handles routine questions and guidance, while educators and staff remain in control for mentoring, grading and sensitive support decisions.

How does pricing work for educational institutions?

Pricing is flexible based on your institution size, usage needs and required features. Zedu offers plans for growing cohorts and custom options for larger schools or multi-programme organisations.
